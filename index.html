New The Mural

Your queue, hung on the wall.

Every queue now opens on a slowly drifting wall of its own cover art. Equal panels laid like brickwork, each row offset by half a panel and gliding calmly in the opposite direction to the one above. It's your backlog, made from the things in it, and it changes with every shelf.

  • Made of your shelf

    Album squares for music and games, posters for film, TV and books. The wall is built from whatever you queued, nothing stock.

  • Calm by design

    A slow, clock-driven drift, softened at the edges. It pauses when you leave the screen and holds perfectly still with Reduce Motion or Low Power Mode on.

  • Ready on launch

    Covers for every shelf are prefetched to disk after launch, so switching from Music to TV never shows an empty wall.

  • Then the queue

    Scroll and the mural gives way to your queue: what's on deck, the next episode, the order you set by hand.

Spin

Can't decide? Spin.

Each shelf has its own spin, built to feel like the thing it picks. Music drops a record on a platter. Games whip a carousel of cases past you. Books flip through a stack. Film pulls a strip through a projector gate. TV surfs channels in a burst of static.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

When can I download Culture Stack?

Culture Stack has been submitted to the App Store for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It's coming soon; the App Store link on this page will work the day it's approved.

Do I need an account?

No. Your library works without signing in and syncs through your own iCloud. You only sign in with Apple if you want the social features.

What is the Mural?

It's the drifting wall of cover art at the top of each queue, built from the items on that shelf. It stays still if you turn on Reduce Motion or Low Power Mode.

Where does the cover art and metadata come from?

Public catalogues, including the Apple iTunes Search API, MusicBrainz, Open Library, TVmaze and TMDB, plus game databases. You can edit any field yourself.

Can I import my existing lists?

Yes. With Pro you can bulk-import a list from Notes or a CSV export from Discogs, Last.fm or RYM.

Who can see my activity?

Only people you approve to follow you, and only what you choose to share. Your queue, notes and unrated items stay private unless you opt in to a public library.

How do I cancel Pro Monthly?

In Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Culture Stack Pro. Lifetime is a one-time purchase and never renews.
